Copy trading, computed not claimed.

A strategist publishes a signed mandate. Followers subscribe with their own wallet. An agent executes it through real order types, refuses when it should, then places the stops it promised. Every fill is a public log, so the track record cannot be inflated by anyone.

The problem. Copy trading runs on numbers you have to take someone's word for. The platform holds your money, the star trader reports their own returns, you find out afterwards.
The fix. The strategy is a hash. The follower keeps their own wallet under revocable delegation. Every fill is public, so returns are derived rather than claimed. A strategist cannot inflate a number anyone can recompute.
What nobody handles. Tokenized equity tickers are squatted. Liquidity is fragmented and salted with pools that quote a price and hold nothing. The underlying equity trades six and a half hours a day while the token trades all day. Mandate refuses on all three rather than filling badly.
The token. $MANDATE is the bond a strategist posts to take followers. It is slashable to the followers who were harmed when the published rules are broken. Launched through Bankr on Robinhood Chain, where creator trading fees pay for the agent's own compute.
